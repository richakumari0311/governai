# Finance Policy: Expense Reimbursement

## 1. Overview

This policy defines reimbursable expense categories, per-category spending limits by employee level, and the approval workflow for reimbursement claims.

## 2. Reimbursement Limits by Category and Level

Monthly reimbursement limits (in USD) by expense category and employee level are as follows.

| Employee Level | Travel (per trip) | Meals (per day) | Client Entertainment (per month) | Home Office (one-time) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Associate | 500 | 40 | 100 | 300 |
| Senior Associate | 750 | 50 | 150 | 300 |
| Manager | 1200 | 60 | 300 | 500 |
| Senior Manager | 2000 | 75 | 500 | 500 |
| Director | 3500 | 100 | 1000 | 750 |

Amounts exceeding these limits require pre-approval from the employee's Finance Business Partner and, for amounts double the limit or higher, from the department head.

## 3. Eligible Expense Categories

Eligible categories include business travel (flights, trains, ground transport), lodging, meals during business travel or client meetings, client entertainment directly tied to a business relationship, and approved home office equipment for remote or hybrid employees. Personal expenses, alcohol beyond client entertainment context, and fines or penalties are never reimbursable.

## 4. Claim Submission

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date via the expense portal, with itemized receipts attached for any single expense over $25. Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and manager sign-off, and claims after 90 days are not eligible for reimbursement except in documented emergencies.

## 5. Approval Workflow

Claims under the level-based limit in the table above are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Claims exceeding the limit route to the Finance Business Partner for review, with a target turnaround of 5 business days. Claims are paid out in the next payroll cycle following approval.

## 6. Corporate Card Usage

Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile all transactions within the same 30-day window as standard claims. Unreconciled transactions after 60 days are flagged to the employee's manager and may result in card suspension.

## 7. Audit and Compliance

All reimbursement claims are subject to random audit. Employees found submitting fraudulent claims are subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination, per the Code of Conduct.
