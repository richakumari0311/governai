# HR Policy: Leave and Time Off

## 1. Overview

This policy governs all categories of paid and unpaid leave for full-time employees. It applies to all levels from Associate through Director. Contractors and interns are covered under a separate policy.

## 2. Leave Entitlement by Level

Annual leave entitlement varies by job level, as shown below.

| Employee Level | Annual Leave (days) | Sick Leave (days) | Carry-Forward Limit (days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Associate | 18 | 10 | 5 |
| Senior Associate | 20 | 10 | 5 |
| Manager | 22 | 12 | 8 |
| Senior Manager | 24 | 12 | 8 |
| Director | 25 | 15 | 10 |

Leave accrues monthly at a rate of entitlement divided by 12. New hires accrue leave from their date of joining, prorated for the first month.

## 3. Sick Leave

Sick leave does not require advance approval but must be reported to the reporting manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence. Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted within 5 business days of return.

## 4. Maternity and Paternity Leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave, in line with statutory requirements. Paternity leave is 2 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 8 weeks of the child's birth or adoption date.

## 5. Approval Process

Leave requests for more than 3 consecutive days require manager approval submitted at least 5 business days in advance. Leave requests of 1-3 days require at least 2 business days notice. Emergency leave is exempt from advance notice but must be reported within 24 hours.

## 6. Unused Leave and Encashment

Leave beyond the carry-forward limit specified in the table above is forfeited at year end, unless local labor law requires encashment, in which case unused days are paid out at the employee's per-day base salary rate.

## 7. Escalation

Disputes regarding leave balance or rejected leave requests should be raised with HR Business Partners. If unresolved within 5 business days, escalate to the HR Policy Committee.
