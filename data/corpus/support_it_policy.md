# Support Policy: IT Helpdesk and Service Levels

## 1. Overview

This policy defines ticket priority levels, response and resolution SLAs, and escalation paths for internal IT support requests.

## 2. Service Level Agreements by Priority

Response and resolution times by ticket priority are as follows.

| Priority | Description | First Response | Resolution Target | Escalation After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 - Critical | Full system outage, security incident | 15 minutes | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P2 - High | Major feature unusable, no workaround | 1 hour | 8 hours | 4 hours |
| P3 - Medium | Feature degraded, workaround exists | 4 hours | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| P4 - Low | Minor issue, cosmetic, general question | 1 business day | 5 business days | 3 business days |

## 3. Ticket Submission

All requests must be submitted through the helpdesk portal or by emailing [email protected], which auto-creates a ticket. Requests made via chat or in person are logged retroactively and are not guaranteed to meet SLA timers until formally logged.

## 4. Priority Assignment

Priority is initially set by the requester and validated by the support agent on first response. Misclassified tickets are re-prioritized, and the SLA clock restarts from the point of re-classification.

## 5. Access Requests

Requests for new software licenses, system access, or hardware are treated as P3 by default unless tied to a P1/P2 incident. Access requests involving finance systems or HR systems require manager approval attached to the ticket before processing begins.

## 6. Escalation Path

If a ticket is not resolved within the escalation window shown in the table above, it is automatically routed to the on-call lead. If still unresolved after double the resolution target, it escalates to the IT Manager. Repeated SLA breaches on the same issue type trigger a root-cause review.

## 7. After-Hours Support

After-hours support is available only for P1 tickets via the emergency on-call line. P2-P4 tickets submitted after hours are queued and the SLA clock begins at the start of the next business day.
